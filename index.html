Cornhole Math

Only the difference counts - and nobody at the barbecue keeps it straight.

Boundary: rules arithmetic only One on the board, three in the hole, cancellation scoring, first to 21. Cornhole Math never rules on a foot foul or a leaning bag - the backyard has its own referees.

The frame truth

Your 2 on the board and 1 in the hole are 5 against their 3 on the board - cancellation leaves 2 for you. The 3 you "scored" was never yours.

The scoreboard truth

15 plus 4 is 19 - 2 from 21. 19 plus 4 is 23, and the game is over before the next drink is poured.

The comeback truth

Down 14, a frame can swing at most 12 - at least 2 perfect frames to catch up. The hole is three points and the hole is the whole plan.
